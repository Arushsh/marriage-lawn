# Content and Client Onboarding Guide

## 1. Client onboarding checklist (ask the owner for these)
**Basic info**
- [ ] Venue name, tagline (optional), logo (or we make a simple text logo)
- [ ] Full address, Google Maps link, nearest landmark
- [ ] Phone, WhatsApp number, email, opening hours
- [ ] Instagram/Facebook/YouTube links

**Venue details**
- [ ] Capacity: seated and floating guests, number of halls/lawns
- [ ] Rooms: guest rooms, bridal room, AC/non-AC
- [ ] Parking capacity, power backup, lift/ramp, washrooms
- [ ] Catering: in-house, outside allowed, any fixed vendors
- [ ] Decoration: in-house, outside allowed
- [ ] DJ/music timing rules, alcohol policy, fireworks policy
- [ ] Advance amount, cancellation policy, what is included in the price

**Pricing**
- [ ] Packages (names, starting price, inclusions) or choose "Get Quote" mode
- [ ] Season/day pricing notes

**Media**
- [ ] 15-25 photos (daytime, evening, decorated events, rooms, dining, entrance)
- [ ] Optional walkthrough video (60-90 sec) or permission to shoot one
- [ ] Permission to use Google reviews

**Calendar**
- [ ] Booked dates for the next 6 months, and who will maintain it (owner or us)

**Legal**
- [ ] Written permission to use their name, photos, and reviews
- [ ] Agreement: scope, price, 50% advance, timeline, maintenance terms

## 2. Copy templates

**Hero headline options**
- "<Lawn Name>: Where Your Wedding Story Begins"
- "<Lawn Name>: A Grand Venue for Your Big Day in <City>"
- "Celebrate Your Special Day at <Lawn Name>"

**Hero sub-line**
"A spacious lawn in <City> for weddings, receptions and engagements, with ample parking and room for up to <N> guests."

**About section (fill in)**
"<Lawn Name> has been hosting weddings in <City> since <year>. With <N>-guest capacity, a beautifully landscaped lawn, <facility>, and a dedicated team, we help families celebrate without stress. Visit us to see the venue and plan your event."

**Enquiry success message**
"Thank you! We have received your enquiry. Our team will call you within 2 hours. For a faster reply, message us on WhatsApp."

**WhatsApp prefilled text**
"Hi, I found <Lawn Name> online. I'm interested in booking for <date> for about <guests> guests. Please share details."

**Calendar disclaimer**
"Dates shown are indicative and may change. Please confirm availability with our team."

**FAQ starter set**
1. What is your capacity? 
2. What is the advance amount to book a date?
3. Can we bring our own decorator or caterer?
4. Until what time is music allowed?
5. Are rooms available for the family and guests?
6. Is parking available, and for how many vehicles?
7. What is the cancellation and refund policy?
8. Do you provide generator backup?
